From "I need this done" to "job complete"

Five stages, the same way every time — so both sides know exactly where things stand.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Search or browse for the service you need and compare providers by price, rating and past work.

  2. 2

    Order & pay

    Pick a package and pay upfront by mobile money, bank transfer or card. Work only starts once payment is confirmed.

  3. 3

    Work gets done

    You and the provider talk through Quick Job's built-in messaging, so there's always a record of what was agreed.

  4. 4

    Delivery & confirmation

    The provider delivers the work. You review it and confirm — or it auto-confirms after a few days.

  5. 5

    Review

    Both sides rate each other, building the track record that helps everyone choose better next time.

Quick Job never touches or holds order money — it moves straight from buyer to provider through our licensed payment partner. The platform earns instead through optional provider subscriptions, and every provider gets full access free for their first six months.

Both sides rate each other honestly after a job. Reviews are checked for abuse and can't be edited afterward.

If buyer and provider disagree, a trained staff member reviews the evidence and aims to resolve it within two business days.

Content moderation

Reports of bad behaviour or fake listings are reviewed by staff, with a clear path from warning to suspension to ban.

A permanent paper trail

Every suspension, refund or decision staff make is logged permanently — nothing happens quietly.
